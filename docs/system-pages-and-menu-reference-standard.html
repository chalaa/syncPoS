syncPoS Documentation

System Pages and Menu Reference

Standard reference for the visible pages, admin menus, submenus, routes, business purpose, and access permissions currently implemented in syncPoS.

Prepared for user acceptance testing and system administration.

1. Access Model

The admin interface uses role-based permissions. A user sees only the modules and submenus allowed by assigned role permissions. Important protected pages and server actions also check permissions on the server.

PermissionPurpose
all:all:allFull access to every module.
catalog:products:viewView product catalog pages.
catalog:products:manageCreate, update, delete, and restore product catalog records.
partners:partners:viewView customers, suppliers, and payment terms.
partners:partners:manageCreate, update, delete, and restore partner records.
inventory:stock:viewView inventory, stock cards, serial history, transfers, and operations.
inventory:stock:receivePost stock operations, purchases, receipts, adjustments, opening stock, and returns.
inventory:locations:manageManage warehouse and shop locations.
sales:orders:createAccess sales order, delivery, invoice, payment, and return flows.
reports:profit:viewView dashboard and detailed reports.
company:settings:manageManage payment configuration, expenses, and company-level operational settings.
iam:users:viewView users.
iam:users:manageCreate, edit, disable, and unlock users.
iam:roles:viewView roles and permissions.
iam:roles:manageCreate, edit, and delete roles.

13. Important Notes

  • The left sidebar and top submenu both use the active user's permissions.
  • Hidden menu items are not the only protection. Important pages and server actions also check permissions.
  • Some planned views are menu routes but not full independent pages yet: dashboard daily activity, dashboard sync status, and operations approvals.
  • Settings Users and Roles support view-only access separately from management access.

15. Page Use Cases

This section explains when each page should be used in daily business operations.

15.1 Dashboard Use Cases

Page/MenuMain Use CaseExample
OverviewMonitor business health from one screen.A manager checks today's sales, payments received, unpaid customer invoices, unpaid vendor bills, and low stock before opening the shop.
Daily ActivityReview operational activity for a specific day.A supervisor checks what was sold, paid, received, or adjusted during the current business day.
Sync StatusMonitor offline device sync readiness and pending activity.A shop manager checks if a display-shop POS device has pending offline sales waiting to sync.

15.2 Product Use Cases

Page/MenuMain Use CaseExample
ProductsMaintain the master product list used by purchasing, inventory, and sales.Add a tractor, spare part, accessory, or consumable so it can be purchased, stocked, sold, and reported.
New/Edit ProductCreate or update product identity, tracking, pricing, taxes, and attributes.Create a serial-tracked machine product with SKU, barcode, brand, unit, sales price, purchase tax, and compatibility notes.
CategoriesGroup products for filtering, reporting, and cleaner catalog management.Create categories such as Tractors, Engines, Tires, Attachments, Filters, or Spare Parts.
BrandsTrack product manufacturer or brand identity.Add brands such as Massey Ferguson, John Deere, Kubota, or local suppliers.
UnitsDefine how product quantity is measured.Use Piece for machines, Liter for oil, Meter for hose, or Set for grouped spare parts.
TaxesConfigure reusable tax rates used on purchase and sales document lines.Create VAT 15%, withholding tax, or tax-exempt configuration and select it on order lines.
Price ListsControl selling prices for different customer groups, shops, or promotions.Use a Retail Price List for walk-in customers and a Wholesale Price List for dealers, with different prices for the same product.
Lots / SerialsTrace unique machines or batches through purchase, stock, sale, return, and warranty.Find the current owner and movement history of a machine using its serial number.

15.3 Partner Use Cases

Page/MenuMain Use CaseExample
All PartnersMaintain all external parties in one place.Register a company that buys spare parts and also supplies used machinery.
CustomersManage people or organizations that buy products.Select customer-only partners during sales order and invoice creation.
SuppliersManage vendors that supply stock or services.Select supplier-only partners during RFQ, purchase order, vendor bill, and supplier payment workflows.
Payment TermsDefine readable payment agreements.Create terms like Cash on Delivery, 30 Days Credit, or 50% Advance and 50% on Delivery.

15.4 Purchasing Use Cases

Page/MenuMain Use CaseExample
RFQs / OrdersControl products being requested or purchased from suppliers.Create an RFQ for spare parts, confirm it as a purchase order, then track receipt and billing.
ReceiptsRecord stock physically received from a supplier.Receive 10 engines from a supplier and assign serial numbers before stock becomes available.
Landed CostsAdd freight, customs, handling, or other capitalized costs to inventory value.Allocate import freight across received machines by value so each machine has a more accurate total cost.
Vendor BillsTrack supplier invoices and payable balances.Record the supplier bill for a purchase order and see remaining balance after partial payment.
PaymentsTrack money paid to suppliers.Register a bank transfer against a vendor bill and reduce the payable balance.
ReturnsSend purchased stock back to a supplier with traceability.Return a damaged serial-tracked machine to the supplier and keep the receipt/return history auditable.

15.5 Sales Use Cases

Page/MenuMain Use CaseExample
Quotations / OrdersPrepare offers and convert approved offers into sales orders.Create a quotation for a customer, confirm it when accepted, then deliver and invoice.
DeliveriesMove sold stock out of inventory through a controlled document.Select the exact serial number of a sold machine and post delivery to reduce stock.
InvoicesTrack customer receivables.Create a customer invoice from a sales order and follow unpaid, partial, or paid status.
PaymentsTrack money received from customers and the account used.Register cash, bank transfer, mobile money, or card payment against a customer invoice.
ReturnsReceive sold goods back from customers and preserve the original sale link.Accept a returned spare part or machine and prepare refund/credit handling.

15.6 Inventory Use Cases

Page/MenuMain Use CaseExample
StockCheck current stock by product and location.Confirm if a product is available in the main warehouse or a display shop before selling.
OperationsReview document-based stock movements.Audit why stock increased or decreased by checking receipts, deliveries, adjustments, scrap, and returns.
TransfersMove stock between warehouses and shops.Transfer a machine from the main warehouse to a display shop with dispatch and receipt control.
AdjustmentsCorrect stock differences discovered during counting.Adjust quantity after physical stock count finds one missing spare part.
ScrapRemove unusable stock from available inventory.Scrap a damaged component so it cannot be sold.
Stock CardTrace a product's quantity changes over time.Investigate all movements for a specific SKU from opening stock through purchases, sales, and returns.
Serial HistoryTrace one serial or lot across its lifecycle.Check where a machine came from, where it was delivered, whether it was returned, and warranty ownership.
LocationsMaintain warehouses, display shops, transit locations, and special locations.Create a new shop location and control whether offline sales or negative stock are allowed there.
Opening StockLoad existing stock before normal purchase/sale workflows begin.Import current warehouse stock from Excel during system go-live.

15.7 Report Use Cases

Page/MenuMain Use CaseExample
Report HubNavigate to all detailed reports.A manager opens the hub to choose sales, stock, payment, receivable, or payable reports.
SalesAnalyze sales performance.Check total sales for this month by customer or document.
ExpensesAnalyze non-inventory operating costs.Review fuel, rent, salary advance, repair, or office expenses by category and date.
Payment AccountsSee which cash/bank/mobile/card account handled each transaction.Reconcile bank transfer payments against the bank statement.
StockReview stock quantity and valuation.Find high-value stock, low stock, and product availability by location.
ReceivablesTrack customer debt.Find customers with unpaid or partially paid invoices.
PayablesTrack supplier debt.Find vendor bills that still need payment.

15.8 Settings Use Cases

Page/MenuMain Use CaseExample
UsersCreate and control login accounts.Create a salesperson user, assign the Salesperson role, or disable a user who left the company.
RolesGroup permissions into reusable access profiles.Create a Branch Manager role that can view inventory, sell products, and view reports.
PermissionsReview the available permission catalog.Check which permission is needed before granting a user access to reports or settings.
PaymentsConfigure how money is received and paid.Create Cash Register, CBE Bank, Telebirr, or Card Terminal accounts and decide inbound/outbound usage.

15.9 Operations Use Cases

Page/MenuMain Use CaseExample
TasksProvide an operational workspace for non-sales/non-purchase work.A supervisor opens operations to review expense or approval-related tasks.
ApprovalsFuture workspace for approval queues.Approve an over-receipt, discount exception, or stock adjustment once approval rules are added.
ExpensesRecord business expenses that should not increase inventory cost.Register rent, transport, utilities, maintenance, or office supplies as expenses.
Expense CategoriesClassify expenses for reporting.Create categories such as Rent, Fuel, Transport, Maintenance, Salary, or Utilities.
